# Chapter 77 Solutions — Key Ideas

- robotics is closed-loop: policy errors change future observations.
- action units/conventions and control-loop timing are part of the ML contract.
- behavior cloning can suffer compounding distribution shift because learned actions visit states absent from expert demonstrations.
- VLA models ground multimodal representations into robot actions but still require deterministic safety/control boundaries.
- success must be reported together with constraint violations, intervention rate and latency.
