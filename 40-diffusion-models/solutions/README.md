# Chapter 40 Solutions — Key Ideas

- alpha_t = 1-beta_t and alpha_bar_t is the cumulative product.
- q(x_t|x_0) can be sampled directly from x_0 and one Gaussian noise tensor.
- epsilon prediction lets the model estimate clean x_0 and parameterize a reverse step.
- diffusion training usually samples a random timestep, while generation iterates across many timesteps.
- classifier-free guidance mixes conditional and unconditional predictions and trades diversity against conditioning strength.
