# Chapter 39 Solutions — Key Ideas

- explicit density models expose or bound likelihood; implicit models may only provide a sampler.
- autoregressive models factorize likelihood into conditional next-step probabilities.
- VAEs optimize an ELBO rather than exact marginal likelihood in general.
- GANs learn through adversarial discrimination rather than direct NLL.
- diffusion models learn to reverse progressive noising, commonly through denoising/score-related objectives.
