# Curriculum Completion Standard v2

This file defines what complete means for this repository. It does not claim that all possible AI research is exhausted; it means the published Zero-to-Elite roadmap and teaching contract are represented.

## Required learning surfaces

Every numbered chapter must provide, directly in its README or through canonical companion materials: why it matters, prerequisites, learning objectives, mental model, core theory, mathematics where applicable, from-scratch mechanism, code walkthrough, visualization or structured inspection, experiments, limitations, analysis workflow, performance reasoning, production perspective, research perspective, common mistakes, exercises, solutions, mini project, interview questions, checklist, summary, next chapter, and references.

For Chapters 13–78, the original README plus SUPPLEMENTARY_EXERCISE_WORKBOOK.md, HARDWARE_LAB_MATRIX.md, source/tests, mini-project, and references together form the complete learning surface.

## Exercise contract
Each chapter exposes at least 20 total exercises. Historical 10-question chapters receive ten additional questions in SUPPLEMENTARY_EXERCISE_WORKBOOK.md.

## Solution contract
Every counted exercise has either a direct solution or an explicit expected-check rubric sufficient to validate the learner's work.

## Hardware contract
Any chapter that trains a model uses HARDWARE_LAB_MATRIX.md before experiments and states local-vs-scaled expectations.

## Reproducibility contract
Record random seed, package/environment information, data split/version, configuration, checkpoint/artifact identity, metric, and relevant hardware.

## Scope honesty
100% complete in repository status means 100% against this documented curriculum contract and roadmap. It never means 100% of all present and future AI knowledge.
